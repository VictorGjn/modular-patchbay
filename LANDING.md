## The Problem

### Every AI tool treats your knowledge the same way.

A customer complaint gets the same weight as your product roadmap.
A six-month-old transcript overrides your latest strategy.
You can't see what's going in. You can't control what comes out.

**You're flying blind.**

The result? AI that parrots customer requests instead of uncovering real needs. Documents that mix speculation with fact. Outputs you can't trust — and can't explain why.

---

## The Insight

### Not all knowledge is created equal.

A signed contract is **ground truth** — it should never be contradicted.
A customer interview is **signal** — it should be interpreted, not repeated.
A competitive analysis is **evidence** — it should be cited and weighed.
Your product strategy is a **framework** — it should shape thinking, not limit it.

**Modular makes this visible.**

Every piece of context gets a knowledge type. Every type gets treated differently. You control the mix — like an audio engineer controls the sound.

## How It Works

### 1. Load Your Context
Drop in your knowledge base — documents, transcripts, research, code. Modular organizes them into channel strips, each with a knowledge type badge.

### 2. Mix Your Signal
Toggle channels ON/OFF. Adjust depth from FULL to MENTION. Watch the token budget in real-time. Select a preset to auto-load the right context for your workflow.

### 3. Choose Your Output
Markdown document? HTML slides? Email draft? Code? Data table? Pick the format — or let Modular auto-detect from your prompt.

### 4. Run with Confidence
Hit RUN. See which sources contributed to which parts. Check the confidence meter. In Expert Mode, every claim links back to its source.

## FAQ

**Q: How is this different from ChatGPT Projects?**
Projects are a box of files — everything goes in at full volume. Modular gives you per-source depth control, knowledge type classification, token budget visibility, multiple output formats, and agent orchestration.

**Q: Do I need my own API keys?**
Yes. Modular is a frontend that calls model APIs on your behalf. Bring your Anthropic, OpenAI, or local model endpoint. Your data stays yours.

**Q: Can I use this with my existing knowledge base?**
Yes. Point Modular at a folder and it auto-scans and classifies your documents. Supports markdown, PDF, code, and text files.

**Q: What's the Knowledge Type System?**
It's a classification layer that tells the AI how to treat each source. Customer feedback (Signal) should be interpreted and unpacked. Product specs (Ground Truth) should never be contradicted. Strategy docs (Framework) should shape but not constrain. This prevents the #1 AI failure mode: treating everything with equal authority.

**Q: Is my data sent to AI providers?**
Only the context you select. You see exactly what goes out (token budget bar) and control every byte (channel strips). Nothing is sent without your explicit RUN.
